## Usability Issues

### Nine-Screen Onboarding  `HIGH`

First-run tour takes nine swipes before any product value is shown; 40% of test users skipped blindly.

> **Recommendation:** Cut the tour to three value-led screens and defer permissions until the moment of need.

### Notification Prompt Too Early  `MEDIUM`

The push-permission dialog fires on first launch; acceptance in the cohort was 9%.

> **Recommendation:** Trigger the prompt after first successful order, framed around delivery updates.

### Unclear Sign-Up Value  `MEDIUM`

Participants could not articulate why an account was needed before browsing.

> **Recommendation:** Allow anonymous browsing and present sign-up at wishlist or checkout with a concrete benefit.

---

## Research Insights

### Skip Behaviour Masks Feature Blindness  `behavioral`

Users who skipped onboarding failed the "find order history" task twice as often — the tour carries real signal when it is short.

### Progress Feedback Reduces Abandonment  `usability`

The prototype with a step indicator held 31% more users through registration than the control.

### Carousel Fails Reduced-Motion Users  `accessibility`

Auto-advancing onboarding cards ignore the OS reduced-motion setting and cannot be paused.

### Personalised First Shelf  `opportunity`

Users who picked two interest categories during onboarding tapped 3× more products in their first session.
