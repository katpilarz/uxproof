## Usability Issues

### Payment Sheet Context Loss  `HIGH`

The native payment sheet hides the order summary; 6 of 26 participants backed out to re-check totals.

> **Recommendation:** Show a persistent one-line total with item count above the payment sheet.

### Address Autofill Mismatch  `MEDIUM`

OS-level autofill fills Polish addresses into the wrong fields, forcing manual correction.

> **Recommendation:** Reorder fields to match platform autofill conventions and support postcode-first lookup.

### Promo Code Friction  `LOW`

The promo field is hidden behind a chevron; users who arrived from a discount campaign missed it.

> **Recommendation:** Auto-apply campaign codes from deep links and confirm with a dismissible banner.

## Research Insights

### Express Checkout Delivers  `usability`

The Apple Pay / BLIK rollout from the Q1 2024 recommendation now carries 34% of mobile orders with a 71% completion rate.

### Mobile Users Buy in Micro-Sessions  `behavioral`

Median mobile purchase spans three app opens; persistent carts and restored state are conversion-critical.

### Saved Baskets Nudge  `opportunity`

A prototype reminder for baskets abandoned 24h earlier recovered 11% in the A/B test cell.

### Dynamic Type Breaks Checkout  `accessibility`

At the largest accessibility font size the pay button renders off-screen; 3.1% of sessions use these sizes.
