## Usability Issues

### Above-Fold Banner Blindness  `MEDIUM`

Heatmaps show the promotional carousel is skipped by 87% of viewers; it occupies the prime viewport slot.

> **Recommendation:** Replace the carousel with the product gallery and move promotions below the buy box.

### Review Trust Deficit  `HIGH`

Participants distrust the uniform five-star wall; verified-purchase labels are missing.

> **Recommendation:** Add verified badges, rating distribution and "most helpful critical review" to the summary.

### Size Guide Modality  `MEDIUM`

The size guide opens a full-page overlay losing gallery context; apparel returns cite sizing at 41%.

> **Recommendation:** Inline the size table in an accordion and add fit feedback from previous buyers.

---

## Research Insights

### Reviews Are the Second Stop  `behavioral`

Gaze sequences show price → reviews within four seconds; review quality directly gates add-to-cart.

### Gallery Zoom Expectations  `usability`

Pinch and double-tap zoom attempts on desktop imagery show users bring mobile gestures everywhere; add click-to-zoom.

### UGC Photos Lift Confidence  `opportunity`

Products with customer photos convert 22% higher in the analytics cohort; prompt for photos post-delivery.

### Star Ratings Lack Text Equivalent  `accessibility`

Screen readers announce decorative stars as unlabeled images; add an aria-label with the numeric rating.
