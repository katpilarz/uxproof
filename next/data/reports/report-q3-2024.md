## Usability Issues

### Dashboard Information Overload  `HIGH`

The landing view shows 14 widgets with equal visual weight; merchants scan for 20+ seconds before acting.

> **Recommendation:** Introduce a task-oriented "Today" panel with the three metrics merchants check every morning.

### Bulk Actions Undiscoverable  `MEDIUM`

Editing 50 product listings requires opening each one; the existing bulk editor was found by 1 of 9 merchants.

> **Recommendation:** Expose bulk selection directly in the listing table with checkboxes and a persistent action bar.

### Ambiguous Order Statuses  `MEDIUM`

Merchants confuse "processing" and "pending" and phone support to clarify; both map to distinct fulfilment states.

> **Recommendation:** Rename statuses in merchant language and add a hover glossary with next-action hints.

## Research Insights

### Merchants Work in Recovery Loops  `behavioral`

Observed sessions revolve around exceptions — failed payments, stuck orders — not the analytics the dashboard leads with.

### Table Density Beats Cards for Pros  `usability`

Power merchants using the dense-table prototype completed inventory tasks 38% faster than with the card layout.

### Alert Digest Instead of Live Widgets  `opportunity`

A morning email digest prototype was rated 4.6/5; it fits the observed "check then act" routine better than dashboards.

### Charts Rely on Colour Alone  `accessibility`

Revenue vs refund lines are distinguishable only by hue; add markers and direct labels for colour-blind merchants.
